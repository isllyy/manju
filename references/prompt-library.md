# Prompt Library

## A. Character Bible / 角色资产总览板

```text
Create a cinematic professional Character Design Asset Sheet / Character Bible,
16:9 widescreen, clean warm-white or cool light-gray studio background,
premium editorial layout, film-production character-development presentation.

IDENTITY:
Use the provided identity reference as the exact identity master reference.
[INSERT CHARACTER DNA + IMMUTABLE VISUAL ANCHORS]

LAYOUT:
Left 30%: large Hero full-body or 3/4 portrait with concise name / role block.
Center top: front full-body, side full-body, back full-body; same height and scale.
Center bottom: three characteristic poses.
Right top: high-resolution face close-up.
Right middle: six detail crops — hair, collar, fabric, accessories, hands/footwear, signature prop.
Right bottom: three to six expression headshots.

STYLE:
cinematic realism, editorial character bible, professional studio photography,
subtle softbox lighting, natural skin texture, accurate anatomy, highly detailed fabric,
clean spacing, premium visual hierarchy, restrained typography.

[APPEND CONSISTENCY RULES]
```

## B. Daily Work Looks / 日常工作形象

```text
Create a 16:9 professional daily-work character portfolio for the same person.
Keep exact identity continuity with the reference portrait.

Show six full-body work looks:
1 meeting presentation — formal business
2 project workshop — light business
3 team leadership — soft professional
4 daily desk work — refined minimal office wear
5 field research — smart casual / practical
6 training or keynote — confident speaker look

Add six work scenarios:
public speaking, team discussion, focused laptop work, internal training,
field research, content creation / camera presentation.

Add an expression strip and selected detail crops.
Visual tone: credible, modern, intelligent, approachable, natural corporate photography.
Not a fashion catalog; each look must feel usable in real work.
[APPEND CONSISTENCY RULES]
```

## C. Daily Life Looks / 日常生活休闲形象

```text
Create a 16:9 refined lifestyle character portfolio for the exact same person.
Preserve identity, age impression, facial structure, hairline and recognizable appearance.

Show six relaxed looks:
city walk, café reading, fitness, home leisure, travel exploration, hobby/social moment.

Show six lifestyle scenarios:
cooking or home routine, laptop journaling, photography, bookstore reading,
friends/café, evening city walk.

Wardrobe: tasteful modern casual, knitwear, trench coat, denim, sneakers,
soft shirts and relaxed tailoring; coherent personal wardrobe, not model cosplay.
Lighting: natural daylight, warm indoor practicals, soft evening ambience.
Mood: curious, grounded, warm, self-possessed, contemporary.
[APPEND CONSISTENCY RULES]
```

## Consistency block

```text
This is one single character shown multiple times, not multiple similar-looking people.
Preserve the same identity, facial bone structure, facial proportions, age impression,
hairline, eyes, nose, mouth, jawline, body proportions, skin tone and immutable anchors.
Do not beautify into a different face. Do not make lookalikes. Do not allow face drift.
```
